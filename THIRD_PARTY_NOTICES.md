## DejaVu fonts

Unmodified DejaVuSansCondensed.ttf, DejaVuSansCondensed-Bold.ttf and
DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf are bundled and embedded in PDF output. They were obtained
from the bundled LibreOffice distribution. The full copyright/license notice
is included at `src/kursove_pdf/fonts/DejaVu-LICENSE.txt` and shipped in the wheel.
The fonts use the Bitstream Vera license, with DejaVu changes in the public domain.
Upstream: https://github.com/dejavu-fonts/dejavu-fonts
License: https://github.com/dejavu-fonts/dejavu-fonts/blob/master/LICENSE
No proprietary Arial font is redistributed.
